\block{Abstract}{
  Astronauts exhibit elevated nephrolithiasis risk post-flight, with prior work linking
  microgravity to distal nephron remodeling and renal transporter dysregulation.
  Whether spaceflight alters \emph{regulatory coordination} among genes---shifting
  co-expression architecture without necessarily changing mean expression---remains
  uncharacterised.

  \vspace{0.5em}
  We analysed OSD-771 (RRRM-2), bulk kidney RNA-seq from \textbf{80 female C57BL/6NTac mice}
  under a $2{\times}4{\times}2$ factorial design crossing \textbf{Age} (Young 16 wks;
  Old 34 wks), \textbf{Arm} (ISS-T; LAR), and \textbf{Environment Group} (FLT, GC, VIV, BSL).
  MuSiC deconvolution removed compositional confounding.  Sample-specific networks via LIONESS
  on a shared sparse skeleton, followed by node2vec embeddings aligned by Procrustes, yielded
  per-gene \emph{rewiring scores} across four primary contrasts.

  \vspace{0.5em}
  Focused permutation testing identified \textbf{11 significantly rewired genes in Young ISS-T}
  (FDR $<0.05$), including \textit{Slc6a20a}, \textit{Cry2}, and \textit{Gc}, with two
  additional hits in Old ISS-T (\textit{Prlr}, \textit{Nr4a1}).  Pathway enrichment revealed
  ECM/complement remodeling in Young ISS-T, EMT/steroid biosynthesis in Old ISS-T, and
  \textbf{retinoic acid metabolism} as the most robust cross-arm signal
  (OR\,=\,19.9, LAR Young).  Rewiring was statistically orthogonal to DE ($\rho \approx 0.03$).
}

\block{Background \& Rationale}{
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Astronauts experience $\sim$5$\times$ elevated kidney stone risk post-flight;
          DCT ion-transport dysregulation is implicated.
    \item Standard DE tests only \emph{mean shifts}; regulatory \emph{rewiring} at stable
          mean expression is invisible to conventional analyses.
    \item RRRM-2 (OSD-771): largest factorial kidney RNA-seq spaceflight dataset, $n{=}80$.
    \item \textbf{Central question:} Does spaceflight restructure the kidney co-expression
          network in an age- and arm-dependent manner?
  \end{itemize}

\block{Statistical Considerations}{
  \textbf{Combinatorial ceiling.}
  With $n{=}5$ FLT and $N{=}20$ total per stratum, $\binom{20}{5}=15{,}504$ permutations
  exist.  Even exhaustive enumeration gives minimum $p \approx 6.4{\times}10^{-5}$;
  after BH over 2,500 genes, minimum $q \approx 0.16$.
  \textbf{This is a mathematical property of the design, not a failure of signal.}

  \vspace{0.5em}
  \textbf{Focused permutation.}
  BH restricted to top rewiring decile (250 genes) is biologically motivated and
  statistically principled—analogous to candidate-gene analysis in GWAS.  Cutoff defined
  by rewiring rank, not by $p$-values, avoiding circular inference.
  Inter-seed CV $<2\%$ across 10 node2vec runs confirms embedding stability.
}

\block{Results: Rewiring Landscape}{
  Mean rewiring ($\Delta$) was similar across all four contrasts (0.269--0.284),
  indicating a \textbf{genome-wide perturbation}.  Maximum single-gene rewiring reached
  0.547—\,\approx twice the genome-wide average—identifying individual hub genes as
  extreme outliers.

  \vspace{0.4em}
  \shadebox{UoMLightBlue!15}{%
    \textbf{Key result:} Spearman $\rho = 0.02$--$0.05$ between $\Delta$ and
    $|\log_2\text{FC}|$ ($R^2 < 0.3\%$) across all contrasts.
    \textbf{Rewiring is statistically orthogonal to differential expression.}%
  }

\block{Integrated Biological Interpretation}{
  \textbf{1.\ Coordinated ECM and Innate Immune Remodeling.}
  ECM organization, collagen trimerisation, complement/coagulation, and integrin signalling
  rewired across contrasts.  Age modulates the signature: ECM/complement in Young;
  EMT/steroid biosynthesis in Old.

  \vspace{0.4em}
  \textbf{2.\ Hub-Gene Rewiring at Topological Bottlenecks.}
  Hits span renal transporters, secretory proteins, metabolic enzymes, circadian regulation,
  and hormonal signalling—\emph{topological hubs} coordinating multiple biological
  processes, not members of a single pathway.

  \vspace{0.4em}
  \textbf{3.\ Retinoic Acid as Convergent Repair Signal.}
  Detected by gene-level and pathway-level inference; strongest in LAR (recovery arm).
  Candidate for mechanistic follow-up and therapeutic targeting.

  \vspace{0.5em}
  \shadebox{UoMGray}{%
    \textbf{Age effect:} Young kidney is more susceptible to individual rewiring events
    (11 hits vs. 2 in Old).  Old kidney may already occupy a perturbed baseline,
    leaving less headroom for additional flight-induced change.%
  }
}

\block{Results: ISS-T vs. LAR and Convergent Retinoic Acid Signal}{
  ISS-T shows \textbf{greater and more significant rewiring} than LAR across all contrasts
  (11 hits vs. 1 in Young; 2 vs. 0 in Old), consistent with \emph{partial network
  recovery} following return to terrestrial gravity.

  \vspace{0.4em}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item $\sim$\textbf{15\%} top-decile genes are \textbf{ISS-T-specific} (ephemeral;
          resolved on return): \textit{Cry2}, \textit{Foxq1}, renal transporters.
    \item $\sim$\textbf{22\%} are \textbf{persistent} (rewired in both arms):
          \textit{Cfh}, \textit{Vwf}, \textit{Col1a1}, \textit{Fn1}.
  \end{itemize}

  \vspace{0.5em}
  \shadebox{UoMLightBlue!15}{%
    \textbf{Convergent signal: Retinoic acid metabolism} is the single most reproducible
    finding. OR\,=\,19.9 in LAR Young ($q=1.6{\times}10^{-5}$); 9/35 pathway members in
    top decile; nominal enrichment in ISS-T Young ($p=0.003$).  Strongest in the LAR arm,
    suggesting a \emph{recovery/repair programme} during re-adaptation. Retinol metabolism is
    essential for tubular differentiation, podocyte maintenance, and renal injury repair.%
  }
}
