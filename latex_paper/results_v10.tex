\noindent
This document collects the curated result narrative, manuscript tables, supplementary tables, main figures, and figure gallery supporting Manuscript v10. Manuscript v10 is framed as a \emph{phenotype-anchored} cross-cohort transcriptomic study: the analysis is hung off a measured molecular endpoint --- suppression of NCC/SPAK regulatory phosphorylation in the distal convoluted tubule --- and organized as an evidence ladder. The compendium follows that claim hierarchy: the original RRRM-2 age-rewiring claim fails at primary resolution; terminal-flight kidney samples define a matrix-high / DCT/NCC-low transcriptomic state; this RNA direction recurs in OSD-513 and again in OSD-462 RNA; OSD-462 protein abundance is null; the WNK--SPAK/OSR1--NCC regulatory phosphorylation cluster is specifically suppressed; kinase-substrate enrichment (KSEA) confirms SPAK/OSR1 and WNK activity loss as a positive control; and an animal-matched comparison within RR-10 links the DCT transport RNA program to the measured NCC regulatory phosphorylation at group level, with a suggestive but underpowered per-animal link. The compendium does \emph{not} claim NCC dephosphorylation as a new discovery; that phenotype was established by the Cosmic Kidney Disease study and is used here as a fixed anchor. All numbers are drawn from the run artifacts \code{run\_20260519\_000547\_2500g}, \code{run\_20260519\_cosine\_perm\_null}, \code{run\_20260519\_5000g\_sensitivity\_3seed}, \code{run\_20260521\_osd462\_anchor}, \code{run\_20260522\_regulator\_activity}, and \code{run\_20260522\_phenotype\_anchor}. The six-recommendation expansion (Section~\ref{sec:sixrec}) adds \code{run\_20260522\_osd462\_anchor} (re-run), the completed decoupler Layer~B, \code{run\_20260522\_celltype\_decomposition}, \code{run\_20260522\_physiology\_assessment}, and \code{run\_20260522\_sixrec\_integration}.

\section{Phenotype-Anchored Methodology}

The complete methodology is summarized in Figure~\ref{fig:method}. Four public mouse kidney spaceflight datasets feed a cross-cohort RNA-state contrast; the OSD-462 / RR-10 multi-omic anchor adds protein and phosphoprotein layers; KSEA quantifies the Tier-1 phenotype as a kinase-activity endpoint; and the animal-matched phenotype-anchoring comparison links the transcriptomic DCT transport program to the measured NCC regulatory phosphorylation. The structure is read as an evidence ladder: RNA state recurrence $\rightarrow$ phospho activity (measured, Tier 1) $\rightarrow$ DCT morphology (Cosmic Kidney Disease, Tier 2 literature) $\rightarrow$ physiology (Tier 3, future work).

\begin{figure}[H]\centering
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{methodology_overview.pdf}

\section{Claim Gates and Hypothesis Outcomes}

The OSD-462 multi-omic anchor was specified in a pre-registered plan (\path{docs/osd462_multiomics_analysis_plan.md}) with four hypotheses, each carrying an explicit falsification rule declared before any protein or phosphoprotein effect was computed. Table~\ref{tab:hyp} records the outcomes. Three hypotheses were falsified and one was strongly supported as a positive-control anchor; under the pre-registration, every outcome --- including the nulls --- is a reportable result.

\begin{table}[H]

\label{tab:hyp}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{>{\bfseries}p{0.06\textwidth}X p{0.16\textwidth}}
\toprule
ID & Hypothesis and pre-registered prediction & Outcome \\
\midrule
H1 & Matrix/ECM transcripts and proteins are concordant in flight (matrix proteins up; concordance exceeds matched null). & \textbf{Falsified.} Matrix proteins moved opposite transcripts. \\
H2 & DCT/NCC-WNK transcripts and proteins are concordant (transporter proteins down; concordance exceeds null). & \textbf{Falsified.} No protein-abundance effect; total NCC protein flat. \\

\bottomrule

\noindent
\textbf{Decision-table outcome.} Protein-abundance concordance was null for both matrix and DCT gene sets; the DCT/NCC side of the RNA state localized to the phospho/activity layer. Per the pre-registered claim ceiling, the result is a molecular claim (transcript-state plus signaling anchor), with fibrosis/tissue deposition, urine chemistry, and causal upstream drivers remaining out of scope.

\section{Regulator-Activity Inference: KSEA}

Kinase-substrate enrichment analysis (KSEA) was applied to the OSD-462 phosphoproteome to express the Tier-1 phenotype as a regulator-activity endpoint. A kinase is scored by the mean Space~Flight minus Ground~Control log$_2$ effect of its annotated substrate phosphosites, standardized against all 21{,}083 quantified sites ($z=(\bar{s}_{\mathrm{kinase}}-\bar{s}_{\mathrm{all}})\sqrt{m}/\sigma_{\mathrm{all}}$). The kinase-substrate relationships are from a small curated, individually cited renal network restricted to the WNK--SPAK/OSR1--NCC chain. This layer is a designed positive control: because the Cosmic Kidney Disease study establishes NCC/SPAK dephosphorylation in flight, a valid implementation must return SPAK/OSR1 and WNK activity down.

The result is not a discovery --- it is the expected consequence of the established phenotype --- but it certifies that the regulator-activity machinery is sensitive and correctly implemented, licensing its use for candidate transcription-factor and pathway nomination (decoupler PROGENy/CollecTRI layer). Layer~B has now been executed within five cohorts; its cross-cohort recurrence verdict is reported in Section~\ref{sec:sixrec}.

\section{Phenotype Anchoring: Animal-Matched RNA-State vs NCC Activity}

OSD-462 / RR-10 acquired kidney bulk RNA-seq and phosphoproteomics on the \emph{same animals}. A per-animal DCT transport RNA score (mean $z$ of the DCT/NCC-WNK transport gene panel) and a per-animal NCC regulatory-phosphorylation activity score (mean $z$ of \gene{Slc12a3} Thr53/58/65/68 and \gene{Stk39} Ser382/383) were matched by animal. Twenty Space~Flight and Ground~Control animals carried both modalities and entered the flight-versus-ground statistics; ten Basal animals were scored for completeness. Both scores are signed so higher means more DCT transport / more NCC-activating phosphorylation.

\section{Six-recommendation integration and honest grades}\label{sec:sixrec}

The six analytical recommendations of the May 2026 strategy memo were run end-to-end and integrated into this phenotype-anchored frame; a self-contained write-up is in \code{results\_sixrec\_integration.tex}. The guiding thesis is unchanged: the established spaceflight DCT/NCC phenotype sits inside a recurrent kidney-wide RNA remodeling context that is \emph{cross-omically decoupled} --- it recurs in transcriptomes, fails as a protein-abundance story, and resolves at the NCC/SPAK regulatory-phosphorylation layer (Figure~\ref{fig:sixrec}).

\begin{figure}[H]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{fig_sixrec_integration.pdf}
\caption{\textbf{Consolidated six-recommendation integration.} (A)~RNA pathway-vector recurrence (RRRM-2 ISS-T vs OSD-462, cosine $0.87$, gate PASS). (B)~Protein-abundance concordance null/opposite (H1,~H2 falsified). (C)~NCC/SPAK activating regulatory phosphosites (asterisk) suppressed while non-regulatory \gene{Slc12a3} sites and total NCC protein are flat (H3 supported). (D)~KSEA WNK/SPAK inferred-down (anchor); decoupler recurrent activity dominated by expected injury programs, with an endothelial/vascular TF module as the most coherent non-obvious recurrent signal. (E)~Marker-panel decomposition: DCT identity \emph{and} transport fall while endothelial/stromal compartments rise; per animal the endothelial score anti-correlates with NCC regulatory phospho ($\rho=-0.76$, $p<0.001$). (F)~Evidence ladder and grades.}
\label{fig:sixrec}
\end{figure}

\noindent\textbf{Regulator Layer~B (Rec~2).} decoupler ULM (PROGENy + CollecTRI, mouse) was run within five cohorts. Across the four terminal-flight cohorts the recurrently-up pathways are the \emph{expected} injury programs (NF-$\kappa$B, TNF$\alpha$, JAK-STAT, Hypoxia), all of which reverse sign in the RRRM-2 LAR live-return arm. Of 751 scored transcription factors, none recur as inferred-down; the most coherent non-obvious recurrent signal is an endothelial/vascular module (\gene{Erg}, \gene{Gata2}, \gene{Ets1}). No single non-obvious driver dominates beyond known injury/stress pathways --- reported as claim discipline, with the endothelial axis nominated as a candidate context axis.

\noindent\textbf{Cell-type decomposition (Rec~4).} A curated marker-panel split shows that across cohorts \emph{both} DCT identity and DCT transport fall while endothelial and stromal compartments rise; per animal the endothelial score anti-correlates with measured NCC regulatory phospho ($\rho=-0.76$, $p=0.0004$). The bulk DCT-low signal therefore carries a genuine interstitial-expansion/composition component and cannot be read as pure transcriptional suppression --- which is exactly why the regulatory-phosphosite layer (composition-independent, $\sim$4$\times$ more suppressed at regulatory than non-regulatory sites) is the more reliable transporter readout.

\noindent\textbf{Physiology (Rec~5).} No animal-level physiology exists in the OSD-462 omics record. The same-mission Cosmic Kidney Disease study supplies a Tier-2 anchor (DCT tNCC density $-47.6\%$ in flight, $p=0.001$) and Tier-3 group-level plasma/urine electrolytes; animal-matched endpoints remain future work.

\section{Verification notes}

The following independent checks were performed. (i) The eight \path{tests/test_osd462_anchor.py} unit tests pass, as do the 15 tests in \path{tests/test_regulator_activity.py} and \path{tests/test_phenotype_anchor.py} (matched-design assertion: phospho channel \code{FL-01} and RNA sample \code{FLT\_F1} resolve to the same animal). (ii) NCC phosphosite flight effects were re-derived directly from the raw \path{siteQuant_360} workbook (log$_2$ of TMT signal-to-noise, 10 FL vs 10 GC channels, Welch $t$-test, no pipeline code): Thr53 $-0.99$, Thr65 $-0.93$ ($p=2.3\times10^{-6}$), Thr68 $-0.94$, Thr89 $-1.07$; non-regulatory Ser96/Ser120/Ser122 flat --- confirming the pipeline output in direction, magnitude, and significance. (iii) Total NCC protein flight effect ($+0.089$, 70 peptides) and the matrix-protein effects were confirmed from \path{protein_effects_gene_anyplex.tsv}. (iv) The RNA recurrence cosine and leave-one-pathway-out range were reproduced from \path{osd462_rna_recurrence.tsv}. (v) The KSEA positive control passes: SPAK/OSR1 $z=-6.31$ and WNK $z=-4.12$, both inferred activity down at $p<0.05$, against a 21{,}083-site background. (vi) The phenotype-anchoring comparison returns concordant group effects (RNA $-0.324$, phospho $-1.298$) with the regulatory cluster suppressed $\sim$4.3$\times$ more than non-regulatory sites; the per-animal correlations are positive but non-significant ($p\geq0.11$), and the non-regulatory-site control wrinkle ($+0.555$, $p=0.011$) is reported rather than suppressed. The site-specific pattern (regulatory cluster down, non-regulatory sites flat, total protein flat) is the principal internal control establishing that the phospho-axis result is genuine activating-site regulation rather than a normalization or abundance artifact.
