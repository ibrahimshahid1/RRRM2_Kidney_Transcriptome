## 0. Executive verdict

v11 is now a **statistically honest, methodologically rigorous reanalysis paper with one defensible primary result and a large, well-bounded ring of negative/contextual findings.** It is a credible preprint and an excellent mentor-facing draft. Its ceiling is set by **novelty**, not rigor: the phenotype it studies (NCC/WNK dephosphorylation + DCT remodeling) was established by Siew et al. 2024 using the *same* OSD-462/RR-10 data the paper re-analyzes. The paper's genuine contribution is **resolution and discipline** — DCT-subtype-prior structure, the RNA→protein→phospho mismatch, and an honest map of what public data can and cannot resolve — not a new phenomenon.

## 1. Statistical robustness — **strong**

The primary enrichment now survives an unusually complete gauntlet, and the places it *fails* are reported.

**Primary result — distal-nephron subtype-prior phosphosite enrichment.** Suppressed (p<0.05, effect<0) whole-kidney phosphosites are over-represented among parent genes at DCT1/DCT2 reference-prior extremes. The DCT1-vs-DCT2 evidence ladder is internally consistent and honestly mixed:

- DCT1-high is strongest at the raw site level (row Fisher OR 1.51, q=1.3×10⁻¹¹; threshold-free MWU p=1.4×10⁻¹²) **but fails the strict tests**: covariate-adjusted parent-gene logistic OR 1.14 (n.s.), and observability-matched parent-gene permutation empirical p=0.107.
- DCT2-leaning is weaker at the raw level (OR 1.29) **but stronger and more robust** in parent-gene-aware tests (one-row-per-gene 1.68; parent-gene Fisher 1.82, q=3.3×10⁻⁸) and is the bin that survives the matched permutation (OR 1.70, p=0.017).
- The covariate-adjusted logistic attenuates **both** (DCT1 n.s.; DCT2 p=0.06), correctly keeping the claim at "parent-gene prioritization," not localization.

**Verdict:** the safe claim is exactly the one the abstract now makes. No single test carries it; the convergence across row, parent-gene, bootstrap, and threshold-free tests — minus the matched-permutation failure for DCT1 — is what supports it.

**TMT channel-loading QC (new, load-bearing).** The reviewer-style concern that the enrichment is a normalization artifact is now directly tested and refuted: raw flight phosphosite channels run ~0.13 log₂ below ground control (protein channels flat at ~0.01), but per-site effects are estimated within-plex after per-channel median centering. Re-running the enrichment on **un**centered effects inflates the suppressed fraction (15%→38%) yet still retains significant enrichment (DCT1 1.27, DCT2 1.17); centered estimates are stronger (1.51/1.29). The enrichment is therefore a relative-ranking result. **This is a genuine strengthening** and converts a latent vulnerability into a documented robustness check.

**Honest nulls preserved (a credibility asset):**
- Continuous DCT1 gradient: **null/opposite** — row Spearman ρ=−0.008 (n.s.); parent-gene ρ=**+0.045 (p=0.001, wrong direction)**. The relationship is threshold-like, not graded; the artifact label now states this.
- Cross-cohort transport recurrence: **not significant** (Stouffer p=0.19, median FDR 0.95, I²=73%); only matrix/ECM recurs (p=7.0×10⁻⁴).
- Continuous DCT contrast: q=0.995.
- Aldosterone/mineralocorticoid axis: meta effect −0.84, p=0.060 (n.s.).
- Human urine concordance: by-axis two-sided sign test p=0.25 (3/3 axes, single trajectory, underpowered).

**Residual statistical caveats (correctly disclosed):** nominal p<0.05 defines a directional *set*, not site-level significance; phosphosite rows are non-independent; the DCT prior is transcriptomic, not phospho; whole-kidney TMT ≠ phospho-stoichiometry. All are in the limitations.

## 2. Biological findings & implications — **sound, one factual error**

The biological story is coherent and well-anchored: a recurrent matrix/endothelial-high, DCT-transport-low **RNA** response that does **not** propagate to protein abundance (matrix proteins move opposite transcripts; total NCC protein flat), with the transporter signal resolving at the **regulatory-phosphorylation** layer (NCC N-terminal sites −0.79 to −0.99 log₂; p65;p68 −1.563, p=9.3×10⁻⁷). This is biologically correct: NCC activity is WNK–SPAK/OSR1-phosphorylation-gated, so flat NCC protein with reduced regulatory phosphorylation is exactly the expected signature of reduced transporter *activity* without abundance loss.

**Corroboration with current literature (strong):**
- **Siew 2024** (Nat Commun 15:4923) independently establishes spaceflight NCC dephosphorylation **and** DCT remodeling/expansion across 24 datasets — the paper's anchor and its remodeling-RNA recurrence both align with Siew's GO/KEGG remodeling findings (cell adhesion, actin organization, epithelial tube morphogenesis).
- **Su 2024** (PMC11000721; the GSE228367 prior source) confirms DCT1/DCT2 are distinct subsegments with the marker structure the paper uses, and that **DCT2 is plastic under K-restriction** — which supports treating the DCT2-leaning bin as biologically meaningful rather than a statistical edge.
- **WNK–SPAK–NCC literature (2024–25)** confirms WNK4→SPAK→NCC as the canonical DCT activation axis.

**Resolved factual check:** an earlier DCT2-biology paragraph risked attributing magnesium handling to DCT2/CNT. Per Su 2024, **magnesium transport is DCT1-predominant (TRPM6), whereas calcium handling is the DCT2 specialty (TRPV5, Calb1).** The current manuscript now describes the DCT2/CNT transition as calcium-handling and electrogenic Na⁺/K⁺ transport context, while keeping magnesium/Trpm6 framed as DCT1-leaning.

**Implications (appropriately hedged):** the work yields a *testable* prediction (DCT-adjacent neighborhoods carry the transport drop in late repair; DCT-marker-high regions retain identity) rather than a functional claim. Correct, given no matched physiology/urine chemistry exists in the public records.

## 3. Models & methods — **appropriate, not overreaching**

- Within-study gene-set z-scoring and within-plex FL−GC phospho effects with per-channel centering: standard and defensible; avoids cross-study pooling.
- Fisher/parent-gene Fisher/logistic/cluster-bootstrap/matched-permutation/threshold-free **family** for one question: this is the right way to stress a non-independent enrichment, and the matched permutation (preserving site count, peptide count, abundance, missingness) is the most honest test in the paper.
- DerSimonian–Laird recurrence meta and Johnson-atlas motif KSEA are standard.
- **Correctly avoided:** causal/SEM language for the covariance decomposition (kept as exploratory OLS), deconvolution claims for composition models, and CLUE-grade language for the local CMap approximation.

No model is doing more work than it can bear. The one place to watch is the parent-gene logistic — because it knocks both bins to (near-)n.s., it is the strongest argument *against* a strong enrichment claim, and the paper rightly lets it stand rather than burying it.

---

## 4. Hypothesis strength — **moderate and honestly scoped**

The operative hypothesis ("flight-suppressed whole-kidney phosphosites prioritize distal-nephron subtype-prior parent genes, with the transporter lesion at the phospho layer") is **falsifiable, partially supported, and correctly bounded.** It is not a strong mechanistic hypothesis (no upstream cause is identified; the public perturbation references — low-K DCT, dDAVP, KLHL3/CUL3 — all came back bounded/null), and the paper says so. The hypothesis is a *prioritization + layer-localization* claim, which the data can actually support, rather than a cell-of-origin or mechanism claim, which they cannot.

---

## 5. Claims, novelty, validity — **valid; novelty is the weak point**

**Validity:** every headline claim is now scoped to what the data support. The Reach D over-reach (p=0.031→0.25) was the one invalid claim at the start of this cycle and is fixed. The kinome discordance (targeted SPAK z=−6.31 from 3 substrates vs unbiased SPAK/OSR1 z=+1.4/+1.6, n.s.) is now stated rather than glossed — a model of how to handle a method disagreement.

**Novelty (the central limitation):**
- *Not novel:* NCC/WNK dephosphorylation (Siew 2024), DCT remodeling/expansion (Siew 2024), matrix/remodeling RNA recurrence (Siew GO enrichment), DCT1/DCT2 subtype structure (Su 2024). The recurrence meta **confirms** Siew rather than extending it.
- *Plausibly novel / incremental:* (a) mapping flight-suppressed phosphosites onto a DCT1/DCT2 **subtype prior** (Siew did not resolve subtype); (b) explicit RNA↔protein↔phospho **mismatch** documentation; (c) the disciplined boundary map of what public multi-omics can/can't resolve; (d) the IRI-derived **spatial prediction**.

**Verdict:** the contribution is a careful, honest *re-resolution* of an established phenotype using overlapping data. A sympathetic reviewer reads this as a solid methods-and-rigor contribution; a critical reviewer asks "what here is not already in Siew 2024?" The paper's best defense is exactly its current framing — resolution + mismatch + bounded negatives + a falsifiable spatial test — plus, ideally, a clearer one-sentence statement of incremental novelty in the introduction.

## 9. Bottom line

The paper is **honest, rigorous, and publishable as a focused reanalysis** — markedly stronger than at the start of this cycle, primarily in calibration and presentation. The data do not contain a bigger result to find — the right next move for a larger claim is wet-lab or spatial validation, not more public-data computation.
