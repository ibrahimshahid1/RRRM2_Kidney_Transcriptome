The owner authorized one final, frozen kidney-compartment audit spanning
podocyte, mesenchymal/stromal, endothelial, TAL, proximal tubule, DCT context,
principal cell, intercalated cell, and immune annotations. The audit must use

and molecular coverage only; they may not be converted into unmeasured disease

## 2026-08-02 compartment-wide adversarial audit resolution

The 51-set, two-sided exact audit is complete. No compartment passed every
frozen adversarial gate. Podocyte-associated parent proteins remain the
strongest follow-up pattern (all-enriched conditional exact p=0.000819), but
the row does not pass the single max-|T| family (FWER=0.324). It otherwise
retains direction across three parent-gene scores, leave-one-gene-out,
uncentered signal, parent-protein subtraction, high-specificity markers, and
the seven-covariate observability null (BH q=0.0455).

Tier-specific mesenchymal/stromal, podocyte, and TAL rows pass the 51-set
family under selected score definitions, but none survives the full declared
cross-checks. Mesenchymal/stromal does not retain convincing scale robustness:
its uncentered statistic attenuates to 0.057 and its parent-protein-adjusted
statistic reverses to -0.171. TAL fails observability matching and contributor
concentration. Podocyte remains blocked by the all-enriched family result and,
regardless of statistics, by condition-to-reporter-block aliasing and lack of
cell origin.

The frozen stop rule is therefore activated: no further OSD-462 compartment
mining will be used to manufacture a biology paper. Podocyte is retained only
as a wet-lab validation target. The defensible repository directions are a
matched-library/design-sensitivity analysis, a provenance/reuse methods note,
or acquisition of urine, histology, physiology, and segment-resolved
validation data.
